About

I'm a Senior Android Developer based in Chandigarh, India, with more than ten years of experience in Kotlin and Java. I currently lead Android for the Marriott Vacation Club, Vistana and Interval International apps, which together serve over 110,000 production users.

My recent work includes moving 100,000+ users from AWS Cognito to PingOne DaVinci with MFA and biometrics, delivering pricing features across exchange and Getaway bookings, and leading an incremental migration from XML to Jetpack Compose. I like owning features from technical design through to production release, and working closely with product, QA, backend and iOS teams.
